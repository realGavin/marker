## Description
Every course you've ever played, on one beautiful map.

Marker Golf is the course collector's app — Letterboxd for golf. Log the
courses you play, rate them, chase bucket lists, and turn your golf life
into a map worth showing off.

EVERY COURSE, ONE MAP
• 12,000+ US golf courses with aerial photos of nearly every one
• Filter by holes, public or private, and distance from you
• Search by course name or by town

LOG YOUR GOLF LIFE
• Mark courses played or want-to-play in one tap
• Rate out of 10, keep private notes
• Chase curated bucket lists — Iconic American Publics, Major venues, and more
• Earn your collector rank: are you top 5%?

SHARE YOUR CARD
• Your played courses become a striking share card
• One tap to your group chat — and yes, it shows your rank

PLAN TRIPS TOGETHER
• Build day-by-day golf trips with real courses — never invented ones
• Invite friends with a code; everyone can see and edit the plan
• Set tee times and get reminded 24 hours and 4 hours before

MARKER PRO
Unlimited personal lists, your stats by state, and the AI Trip Planner that
builds custom itineraries from real courses in our directory.
$5.99/month or $39.99/year with a 7-day free trial.

Privacy first: no ads, no tracking, delete your account any time.
